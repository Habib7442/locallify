# Project Overview

Locallify is a high-performance marketing website designed to get local Indian businesses found on Google and convert searches into WhatsApp inquiries. It positions itself as a premium digital storefront builder ("Made in the North East") for tier-2 and tier-3 city legends.

## Goals
1. **Convert visitors to WhatsApp inquiries** — Primary CTA on every page.
2. **Establish credibility** — Showcase case studies, live examples, and founder presence.
3. **Rank for local-business SEO terms** — Target long-tail tier-2 city queries.
4. **Be share-worthy** — Design a screenshot-worthy, "Voltage" aesthetic site.

## Core User Flow
1. **Discovery:** User lands on Home or a City/Industry landing page via Google or social.
2. **Engagement:** User sees "Voltage" design, reads outcome-focused headlines, and browses live examples.
3. **Validation:** User checks pricing and case studies to confirm value.
4. **Conversion:** User clicks the primary WhatsApp CTA ("Claim your page").
5. **Onboarding:** User interacts with the team via WhatsApp to start their 48-hour build.

## Features
### Core Marketing
- **Hero Showcase:** High-impact "Voltage" hero with live device mockup.
- **Work Gallery:** Filterable grid of customer pages and case studies.
- **Bento Grid:** Interactive explanation of services (The Page, The Presence, The Lead Engine).
- **Pricing:** Clear three-tier comparison table.

### SEO & Content
- **City Pages:** Landing pages optimized for specific Indian cities.
- **Industry Templates:** Vertical-specific showcases (Salons, Gyms, etc.).
- **Blog Engine:** SEO-optimized long-form articles.

### Conversion
- **Floating WhatsApp CTA:** Sticky conversion point.
- **Industry-specific Lead Magnets:** Targeted CTAs on template pages.

## In-Scope
- Full marketing site rebuild with Next.js 15.
- "Voltage" design system implementation.
- Automated SEO (Sitemaps, Schema.org).
- Performance optimization (Lighthouse 95+).
- Blog and Case Study CMS integration.

## Out-of-Scope
- Customer dashboard (this is a marketing site, not the builder itself).
- Direct online payment (all sales handled via WhatsApp/Sales).
- Complex WebGL/3D animations (focus on performance).

## Success Criteria
- [ ] Lighthouse score 95+ on all core pages.
- [ ] Primary WhatsApp CTA clicks tracked and optimized.
- [ ] Mobile-perfect experience on mid-range Android devices.
- [ ] 14 core page templates live and functional.
